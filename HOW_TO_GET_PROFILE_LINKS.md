## 🔗 How to Get Your Social Media Profile Links

### 1. **GitHub Profile Link**

**If you already have a GitHub account:**
1. Go to https://github.com
2. Log in to your account
3. Click on your profile picture (top right)
4. Your profile URL will be: `https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME`
   - Example: `https://github.com/ajithkumar` or `https://github.com/ajith-ak`

**If you don't have a GitHub account:**
1. Go to https://github.com
2. Click "Sign up"
3. Create a free account
4. Your profile URL will be: `https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME`

**To find your exact username:**
- After logging in, click your profile picture → Your profile page shows your username in the URL

---

### 2. **LinkedIn Profile Link**

**If you already have a LinkedIn account:**
1. Go to https://linkedin.com
2. Log in to your account
3. Click on "Me" (your profile picture) in the top menu
4. Click "View Profile"
5. Your profile URL will be in the address bar:
   - Format: `https://www.linkedin.com/in/YOUR-PROFILE-NAME/`
   - Example: `https://www.linkedin.com/in/ajith-kumar-r/`

**To customize your LinkedIn URL:**
1. Go to your profile
2. Click "Edit public profile & URL" (on the right side)
3. Under "Edit your custom URL", you can change it to something like:
   - `linkedin.com/in/ajith-kumar-r`
   - `linkedin.com/in/ajithkumar`

**If you don't have a LinkedIn account:**
1. Go to https://linkedin.com
2. Click "Join now"
3. Create your account
4. Complete your profile
5. Your profile URL will be shown on your profile page

---

### 3. **X (Twitter) Profile Link**

**If you already have an X/Twitter account:**
1. Go to https://x.com or https://twitter.com
2. Log in to your account
3. Click on your profile picture (top right)
4. Click "Profile"
5. Your profile URL will be in the address bar:
   - Format: `https://x.com/YOUR_USERNAME` or `https://twitter.com/YOUR_USERNAME`
   - Example: `https://x.com/ajithkumar` or `https://twitter.com/ajithkumar`

**To find your username:**
- It's the part after `x.com/` or `twitter.com/` in your profile URL
- You can see it in your profile settings

**If you don't have an X/Twitter account:**
1. Go to https://x.com
2. Click "Sign up"
3. Create your account
4. Choose a username
5. Your profile URL will be: `https://x.com/YOUR_USERNAME`
